All about our beer

A family brewery, two generations in

RedDot started with Ernest Ng's homebrewing hobby and a hunch that Singapore was ready for fresh, local beer. In 2007 it became one of the island's first craft breweries.

Today the brewhouse is run by his daughter, Crystalla Huang, who trained in brewing science at VLB Berlin and designed the current brewery herself.
