Why our beer tastes different

Most mass-produced beer is filtered and pasteurised so it survives a long trip. Ours doesn't have to travel. We brew in small batches, skip the filtering and pasteurising, and pour it a few metres from where it was made.

That keeps more of the flavour and aroma in the glass. Getting an unfiltered beer to pour clear takes patience and good brewing fundamentals, and that's what our brewmaster Crystalla Huang has spent more than a decade on.
