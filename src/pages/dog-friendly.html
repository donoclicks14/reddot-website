A dog-friendly garden restaurant at Dempsey

Bring the dog, the kids and the grandparents. Our airy, open-ventilated restaurant has been a family favourite since 2007, and four-legged guests are always welcome.

Dogs welcome, indoors and out

Your dog can join you at the table, inside or in the garden. The space is open, shaded and naturally ventilated, and the greenery keeps things calm for pets and their owners.

We even host dog birthday parties. Ask our events team about booking a corner of the garden for your dog's big day.
