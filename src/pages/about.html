A family brewery, two generations in

RedDot BrewHouse has been brewing in Singapore since 2007, one of the first craft breweries on the island. It's still run by the family that started it.

{{breadcrumb}}

  1. A homebrewing hobby

    Ernest Ng's love of homebrewed beer turns into a plan to bring fresh, local beer to Singapore.

  2. 2007: RedDot opens

    RedDot BrewHouse becomes one of Singapore's pioneering craft breweries, and a family-friendly favourite from day one.

  3. The next generation

    Ernest's daughter, Crystalla Huang, trains at VLB Berlin and takes over as brewmaster and distiller.

  4. Dempsey Hill today

    A garden brewery and restaurant, a distillery making Hidden Hills gin and whisky, and two golds at the 2024 World Beer Awards.

Meet the brewmaster

Crystalla Huang shares her father's love of experimenting. She designed RedDot's current brewery and leads everything from the core range to collaboration brews.

  • Certificate in Brewing Science, VLB Berlin
  • BA in Culinary Arts, The Culinary Institute of America, California
  • Diploma in Food Science and Technology, Temasek Polytechnic
  • General Secretary, Singapore Brewers Association

"Brewing isn't just about beer. It is about a passion for life, and for the greater community."

Crystalla Huang, brewmaster

A space for everyone

Bright with natural light, wrapped in greenery and open to the breeze, RedDot works as well for a business lunch as it does for a date night, a family weekend or a Friday with the band.

Dogs have always been welcome too.
