How a project runs

From first idea to finished cans or kegs, one team handles it.

  1. Recipe development

    Start from your idea or one of ours. We work on ingredients and flavour with you until it's right.

  2. R&D and trial batches

    Test brews to dial in the taste, before anything goes into full production.

  3. Full-scale production

    Brewed in our facility with strict quality control and consistency from batch to batch.

  4. Packaging and labels

    Exclusive recipes, label designs and packaging for retail, events or your own house pour.

What we can brew

  • Craft beer in any style
  • Low-ABV beer
  • Hard seltzer
  • Ready-to-drink cocktails
  • Culinary beers

Our brewery was designed by brewmaster Crystalla Huang to American Society of Mechanical Engineers requirements, and we've been brewing commercially in Singapore since 2007.
